## My Project Overview

Frozen Trust is a local ice-supply business based at 210 Thabana, Section C, Mpumalanga. The business supplies packaged ice to households, event organizers and local businesses, with a focus on dependable service and affordable bulk options. The 2026 promotional material highlights 2 kg and 3 kg ice bags as well as a freezer-and-ice promotional deal, giving the business a practical opportunity to make its products and services easier to discover online. 


---


## Vision and Mission

Mission: To provide affordable, clean, high-quality packaged ice and reliable freezer rental services while delivering excellent customer service. 

Vision: To become a trusted local ice supplier with a strong digital presence and a wider customer reach. 

## Target Audience

Households who need ice for parties, family gatherings, and celebrations. 

Event organizers requiring bulk ice and freezer rentals. 

Restaurants, taverns, shops, supermarkets, and other local businesses that need regular ice supply. 

## Website Goals and Objectives 

## Specific Goals 

To create a professional online presence that introduces Frozen Trust and its services to customers 24/7. 

Make products, prices, promotions, contact details and service information easy to find. 

Generate more enquiries and orders through prominent “Order / Enquire Now” calls-to-action, WhatsApp and an online enquiry form. 

Promote bulk purchasing and seasonal offers, including the 2026 Summer Deals, without depending on a printed flyer alone. 

Build trust by presenting real business information, photographs, clear pricing, and simple customer communication.

 ## Current Website Analysis 

Frozen Trust does not currently have an official website. Its existing promotional approach relies on flyer, direct communication, WhatsApp, and word-of-mouth. These methods are useful for reaching existing customers, but they do not provide one permanent online location where a new customer can quickly compare products, see promotions, learn about services, and submit an enquiry. 

## Strengths 

Strong local customer relationships. 

Quality products at affordable prices. 

Has a professionally designed logo 

The business has straightforward products and services that can be presented through WhatsApp Statues and Facebook. (Frozen Trust,2026) 

## Weaknesses 

There is no official website for customers to browse products and services. 

Customers may need to contact the business directly to obtain information that could be displayed online. 

Business depends mainly on WhatsApp and word-of-mouth marketing. 

## Opportunities for Improvement 

The proposed website will: 

Display products and prices professionally. 

Provide contact information and enquiry forms. 

Improve business credibility. 

Allow customers to request freezer rentals online.
